## Description (4000)
Most of us can only truly keep up with a handful of people. Rooted In helps you look after those relationships on purpose — not by scrolling, but by staying in touch with the people who matter most.

YOUR CIRCLE, IN LAYERS
Rooted In is built around Dunbar's research on how many relationships we can meaningfully maintain. Place each person in one of four layers — Intimate, Close, Active and Meaningful — so you can see at a glance who is closest to you and who you may be drifting from.

GENTLE NUDGES, NOT NOTIFICATIONS FOR THEIR OWN SAKE
Set how often you'd like to stay in touch with each person. Rooted In keeps track of when you last connected and quietly reminds you when it's been a while — and never forgets a birthday.

MEMORIES YOU SHARE
Capture the moments that matter: a dinner, a trip, a phone call that changed your week. Add photos and tag the people who were there, and they can add their own perspective, so a memory becomes something you hold together.

KEEP TRACK OF EVERY CONNECTION
Log calls, catch-ups and voice notes on each person's timeline. Connect your calendar to spot time spent together, and match your contacts to bring the people you already know into your circle — always by your choice, never automatically.

AROUND THE WORLD
See where the people you love are and what time it is for them before you call.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads, ever, and we never sell your data
• No public posts — only the people you choose can see your memories
• You decide which circle layer can see each memory
• Export everything you've created at any time, and delete your account in the app
• Report and block tools, and photos checked for safety

Rooted In is for adults aged 18 and over.
